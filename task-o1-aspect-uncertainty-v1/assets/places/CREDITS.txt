BIRTHPLACE INDEX - DATA CREDITS

This folder is the place index the birthplace search reads (frontend-app/src/app/location/openPlaceIndex.ts). It is built, offline, by
frontend-app/scripts/places/buildPlaceIndex.mjs from two open datasets. How, and what is left out: docs/data/OPEN_PLACE_DATA.md.

1. GeoNames gazetteer - settlements, their names, coordinates, populations and time zones
   https://www.geonames.org/
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Files used: cities500, SK, CZ, admin1CodesASCII, countryInfo and the Slovak alternate names, from
   https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ (dump of 2026-10-23).
   Changes made: places filtered (populated places only), names romanised for search, coordinates rounded to four decimals,
   alternate names reduced to romanised aliases, the data cut into shards. GeoNames does not endorse this use.

2. OpenStreetMap - maternity facilities (the "facilities.json" file)
   (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
   Licence: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
   Extract used: Geofabrik, slovakia-261022.osm.pbf, https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/slovakia.html
   Changes made: objects selected by the rule in docs/data/OPEN_PLACE_DATA.md ("What counts as a maternity facility"), named and tied to
   the nearest settlement. The result is a Derivative Database under the ODbL: it is shared under the same licence, and this file is its credit.

Nothing in this folder is a statement that a facility existed, or had a maternity ward, on any day in the past: it is what OpenStreetMap
says today. No data here comes from Google or from any service that forbids storing it.
