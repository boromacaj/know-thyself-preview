The Moon's surface in KNOW THYSELF PRO: credits.

lroc_color_2k.jpg  - colour map of the Moon, 2048 x 1024, equirectangular (centre longitude 0, north up).
ldem_3_8bit.jpg    - elevation map of the Moon, 1024 x 512, 8 bit, used only to give the surface its relief under the Sun.

Both files are unmodified, as published, from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, "CGI Moon Kit"
(https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), by Ernie Wright (NASA/GSFC SVS). The maps are made from data
assembled by the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LROC wide-angle colour mosaic; LOLA laser altimetry).

Please give credit to: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.  NASA imagery is not copyrighted and may be used freely
with credit (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Nothing is fetched from NASA at run time;
the files are served from the application's own origin.
